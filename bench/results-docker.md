# JWT decode benchmarks

Generated 2026-10-09 from `bench/results-docker/` by `bench/compare.py`, on INTEL(R) XEON(R) PLATINUM 8573C (x86_64), Linux.
Run in Docker, one container per library, each limited to 1 CPU (pinned) and 500 MB with no swap.

Each decode verifies an HS256 signature and checks `exp` and `aud`, as a real caller
would. Times are µs per decode, the mean over the fastest of 5 batches (lower is
better); `(Nx)` is the speed-up vs pyjwt 2.15.1.
